# Content Strategy

## Launch principle
Launch with a small number of honest, high-signal pages and posts.

## Minimum launch content
- Home page copy
- About page copy
- Projects page with at least 3 entries
- 2 technical posts
- 2 42-focused posts or highly structured stubs backed by real repo evidence

## Long-term content buckets
- 42 assignments
- personal projects
- setup/dev environment notes
- retrospectives
- experimental notes

## Tone
- honest
- reflective
- technical but readable
- not inflated

## Writing rule
Prefer:
- what the problem was
- what constraints existed
- what you built
- what failed
- what you learned

Over:
- generic tutorials
- fake polish
- buzzword-heavy writing
